ProjectOverview
Clarity is a web-based supply chain finance platform built to solve a measurable, documented cash-flow crisis faced by Bangladeshi SMEs â€“ typically local suppliers of goods to large corporations. These suppliers wait 90 to 120 days to receive paymenton confirmed invoices, while borrowing from informal moneylenders at over 31% annually just to keep operations running. The platform connects three types ofverified users â€“ Suppliers, Buyers, and Funders â€“ on a single digital marketplace. Suppliersuploadconfirmedinvoicesandreceiveupto97%oftheinvoicevalue within 72 hours at a small discount. Buyers confirm invoices digitally and manage their payables without disrupting their own cash flow. Funders (banks, NBFIs, or institutional investors) browse verified invoices and earn 15 to 20% annualizedreturns on short-term, asset-backed receivables. Clarity earns a 0.5 to 1% facilitation fee on every funded invoice, creating a fully self-sustaining business model.
